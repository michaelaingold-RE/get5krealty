ListingTrueplan Support

ListingTrueplan is an iPhone app from Ingold Group Properties, LLC (5K Realty).

What the app does

Walk a home with a LiDAR iPhone Pro or iPad Pro and leave with an empty 3D model of each floor, a room-by-room list, and approximate square feet. Measure twice, list once.

  • Scans rooms with your device's LiDAR Scanner using Apple's RoomPlan.
  • Makes an empty 3D model (USDZ file) of each floor. Furniture and other objects come out. Walls, doors, windows, and stairs stay in when the scan picks them up.
  • Lets you view the model in the app, or share it by AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or any app in your share sheet, with a note that lists the living area, room count, and number of floors.
  • Gives you a room list with approximate square feet, a living-area total that leaves out the garage, and a gross total of everything measured.
  • Makes a PDF listing packet with those totals and a draft plan for each floor.

Get ListingTrueplan on the App Store

What you need

  • An iPhone 12 Pro or any later Pro or Pro Max model, or an iPad Pro with LiDAR.
  • iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
  • Standard iPhone models, iPhone Air, and Macs can't scan. The app tells you if your device can't.

How to scan a floor

  1. Tap New listing. Add a listing name, an address if you want one, and pick the building type: single story, one and a half, condo, townhome, or multi story. Tap Create.
  2. Open the listing and tap Walk this floor.
  3. Pan the walls of the first room. Step through the doorway and tap Next room. Repeat for each room on that floor.
  4. After the last room, tap Finish floor. Check the room names, then tap Add floor to listing.
  5. Tap View 3D to look at the model, Share 3D model to send it, or 2D packet (draft) for the PDF.

A few tips:

  • Stay on one floor per walk. For a second story, pick that floor and walk it on its own. Walking a floor again replaces that floor only.
  • Have a basement? Tap Add basement, then walk it like any other floor.
  • Rather go one room at a time? Tap Scan one room instead.
  • Room a bit off? Open Advanced to type in a width and depth, square the room up, or nudge and rotate it on the plan.
  • If the rooms don't join up, the app lets you Rescan or place rooms by hand.

About square footage

Square feet from a phone scan are approximate. They are not a survey, an appraisal, or an official measurement. Verify them independently before you publish square footage, and follow your MLS and brokerage rules.

Your scans stay on your device

No account and no sign-in. The app doesn't upload your scans. Listings, 3D files, and PDFs stay on your device until you share them. Details are in the ListingTrueplan Privacy Policy.

Pro subscription

ListingTrueplan is free to download. Pro is an optional subscription at $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year (US prices). Apple bills it, and it renews until you cancel.

  • Cancel: on your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, pick the subscription, and tap Cancel Subscription. See Apple's page: Cancel a subscription from Apple.
  • Already subscribed but Pro isn't showing? Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds: Apple handles all App Store refunds, so we can't issue them. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. See Apple's page: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

Common questions

Which iPhones and iPads can scan?

You need a device with a LiDAR Scanner: iPhone 12 Pro or any later Pro or Pro Max model, or an iPad Pro with LiDAR, on iOS 17 or later. Standard iPhone models, iPhone Air, and Macs can't scan. If your device can't scan, the app tells you when you try to start one.

How accurate is the square footage?

It's an estimate from a phone scan. It is not a survey, an appraisal, or an official measurement. Measure and verify it yourself before you publish square footage, and follow your MLS and brokerage rules.

Where did my furniture go?

The app takes furniture and other objects out of the 3D model on purpose, so people see the rooms, not the sofa. Walls, doors, windows, and stairs stay in when the scan picks them up.

Does the app upload my scans?

No. There is no account and no sign-in, and the app doesn't upload your scans. Listings, 3D files, and PDFs stay on your device until you share them.

Is ListingTrueplan free?

Yes, it's free to download. Pro is an optional subscription at $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year (US prices), billed by Apple.

How do I cancel Pro?

Apple handles subscriptions. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, pick the subscription, and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple's steps are here: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store refunds, so we can't issue them. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's steps are here: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.
