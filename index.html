Why work with 5K Realty

I opened 5K Realty to do the work without the usual percentage commissions. Full service. Same work. More equity left for the seller. The builder years are the useful part: pricing a home so it sells is the job.

The fee

$5,000 or 1%, whichever is greater, paid at closing. Full Canopy MLS listing, professional photos, contracts, and negotiation, start to close. A buyer’s agent commission is optional and up to you. The savings are still significant next to a more traditional listing, and each plan is tailored to the home.

The background

Licensed since 2004. More than 20 years with builders and developers. I spent years representing big builders on new construction pricing, so pricing is where I start. We talk through a realistic high and low range, then set the list price based on your goals and timeline.

The market

I live here. The Greater Charlotte area, NC/SC: South Charlotte, Ballantyne, Fort Mill, Tega Cay, Lake Wylie, Clover, Rock Hill, Indian Land, and York, Lancaster, and Chester County.
