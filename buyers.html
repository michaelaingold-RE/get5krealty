Buyer Agent Fees

In the traditional real estate model, buyer representation is typically paid by the seller through a commission offered in the listing.

When you work with 5K Realty as a buyer, we follow this same structure whenever possible. If the seller is offering compensation to the buyer’s agent, that is how we are paid.

If the seller does not offer compensation, our fee for buyer services is $5,000 or 1% of the purchase price, whichever is greater.
