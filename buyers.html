Get to know the areas

Charlotte→

Known for: Little Sugar Creek Greenway • Park Road Park lake and trails • Carowinds theme park

Drive to Uptown from the Park Road Park area: about 16 to 28 minutes in light traffic, often 18 to 40 minutes at rush hour.

On Charlotte's south side, the Little Sugar Creek Greenway runs more than 17 miles from NoDa all the way to the South Carolina state line. Park Road Park, one of south Charlotte's busiest parks, has a lake, about three miles of mountain bike trail, and a lakeside pavilion. For a bigger day out, Carowinds has rides and the Carolina Harbor Waterpark.

Fort Mill→

Known for: Historic Main Street • Baxter Village and Kingsley • Anne Springs Close Greenway

Drive to Uptown Charlotte: about 22 to 35 minutes in light traffic, often 40 minutes to 1 hour 40 minutes at rush hour.

Fort Mill pairs a historic Main Street with two walkable town centers near I-77 Exit 85: Baxter Village, with Main Street-style shops and restaurants, and Kingsley, with dining, a hotel, and offices that nod to the town's textile past. The 2,100-acre Anne Springs Close Greenway has trails for hiking, biking, and horseback riding, plus lakes for paddling. Walter Y. Elisha Park, a short walk from downtown, hosts the South Carolina Strawberry Festival, and The Peach Stand on Highway 160 West has sold local produce since 1980.

Tega Cay→

Known for: Lake Wylie shoreline • Windjammer Park beach • 27-hole golf club

Drive to Uptown Charlotte: about 22 to 40 minutes in light traffic, often 40 minutes to 1 hour 40 minutes at rush hour.

Tega Cay sits a few miles south of the state line with several miles of shoreline on Lake Wylie, and its name is Polynesian for "beautiful peninsula." Windjammer Park has a lake beach and swimming area, and Catawba Park covers 62 acres with ball fields, walking trails, and a river launch. Tega Cay Golf Club has 27 holes on more than 200 acres around the lake.

Lake Wylie→

Known for: About 325 miles of shoreline • Ebenezer Park beach and campground • Paddle launch at McDowell

Drive to Uptown Charlotte: about 28 to 55 minutes in light traffic, often 50 minutes to 1 hour 40 minutes at rush hour.

Lake Wylie is a Duke Energy lake on the Catawba River, right on the line between North and South Carolina, with about 325 miles of shoreline. Public spots include York County's Ebenezer Park for swimming, fishing, and camping, and Mecklenburg County's McDowell Nature Preserve on York Road, with trails and a launch for kayaks and canoes.

Rock Hill→

Known for: Old Town and Fountain Park • Glencairn Garden • Rock Hill Velodrome

Drive to Uptown Charlotte: about 30 to 45 minutes in light traffic, often 45 minutes to 1 hour 50 minutes at rush hour.

Rock Hill sits along I-77 about 20 miles south of Charlotte, and its historic Old Town is centered on Fountain Park, home to a fountain that reaches over 100 feet high, food truck nights, and a farmers market. Glencairn Garden started as a backyard garden in 1928 and is now 11 acres of lawns, walkways, and spring azaleas. Along the Catawba River, Riverwalk has trails, a BMX track, and the Rock Hill Velodrome.

Indian Land→

Known for: Walnut Creek Park • Bailes Ridge Nature Trail • Del Webb Library

Drive to Uptown Charlotte: about 26 to 45 minutes in light traffic, often 35 minutes to 1 hour 20 minutes at rush hour.

Indian Land is in Lancaster County, with Charlotte Highway running through it past the Del Webb Library and the Indian Land Recreation Center. Walnut Creek Park covers 60 acres with ball fields, tennis courts, two playgrounds, and a 3.5-mile walking trail, and the Bailes Ridge Nature Trail offers a quieter walk off Highway 160.

Ballantyne→

Known for: The Bowl at Ballantyne • Ballantyne's Backyard trails • Concerts at TD Amp

Drive to Uptown Charlotte: about 20 to 26 minutes in light traffic, often 26 to 55 minutes at rush hour.

Ballantyne is in south Charlotte, where The Bowl at Ballantyne gathers restaurants, shops, and rooftop decks around a central green. Ballantyne's Backyard turned a former golf course into more than 100 acres of open space with walking and running trails and catch-and-release ponds. Its paths connect to The Bowl and to TD Amp Ballantyne, an outdoor venue for concerts, festivals, and markets.

York & Lancaster County→

Known for: Kings Mountain State Park • Historic Brattonsville • Andrew Jackson State Park

Drive to Uptown Charlotte from York or Lancaster: about 45 minutes to 1 hour 10 minutes in light traffic, often 1 to 2 hours at rush hour.

Beyond the towns, York and Lancaster counties hold some of the region's biggest outdoor and history sites. Kings Mountain State Park has two fishing lakes and more than 20 miles of hiking trails, Historic Brattonsville is an 800-acre living history site with a Revolutionary War battlefield, and Andrew Jackson State Park near Lancaster has a museum, a fishing lake, and nature trails. In Clover, the town's Main Street program centers on its historic downtown.

Area questions

How long is the drive from Fort Mill, Lake Wylie, or Indian Land to Uptown Charlotte?

In light traffic, plan on about 22 to 35 minutes from downtown Fort Mill, 28 to 55 minutes from Lake Wylie, and 26 to 45 minutes from Indian Land. At weekday rush hour, those trips often take 35 minutes to 1 hour 40 minutes, depending on the route and the day.

Which of these areas are in South Carolina, and which are in North Carolina?

Fort Mill, Tega Cay, Rock Hill, and the Lake Wylie community are in York County, South Carolina. Indian Land is in Lancaster County, South Carolina. Charlotte and Ballantyne are in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina. Lake Wylie itself sits on the state line, so its shoreline is in both states.

Buyer Agent Fees

In the traditional real estate model, buyer representation is typically paid by the seller through a commission offered in the listing.

When you work with 5K Realty as a buyer, we follow this same structure whenever possible. If the seller is offering compensation to the buyer’s agent, that is how we are paid.

If the seller does not offer compensation, our fee for buyer services is $5,000 or 1% of the purchase price, whichever is greater.

Our approach: I help you compare homes and make a clear decision, based on what you asked for and what is actually on the market.
