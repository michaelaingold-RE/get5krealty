Privacy policy for Shingle: The Seller Assist App

Draft date: Oct 15, 2026. This covers the Shingle web app (get5krealty.com/shingle/app/) and the Shingle page (get5krealty.com/shingle/).

Who makes Shingle: 5K Realty (Ingold Group Properties, LLC), a licensed real estate brokerage.

The short version

  • Most of what you enter in the app stays on your device.
  • Shingle sends information in three cases only. When you confirm your phone number, the number goes through our server on Cloudflare to Twilio Verify, which texts you the code. When you accept the disclaimer, a receipt of your acknowledgement goes to 5K Realty through Formspree, our form service. When you send the "Talk to 5K Realty" form, it also goes to 5K Realty through Formspree.
  • Some pages (the Shingle page, the Shingle terms, and the disclosures page) use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies. The app itself blocks it.
  • We don't sell your information, and there are no ads.

What the app stores on your device

  • Everything you enter is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. That includes your name, ZIP code, NC or SC choice, property details, showing times, buyer checklist marks, offers you type in, reply templates, listing text, timeline dates, yard sign settings, your last backup date, and the disclaimer version you accepted and when.
  • We don't receive any of it.
  • Offline cache: the app saves its own files (code, styles, fonts, and images) on your device with a service worker, so it opens without a connection. That cache holds no personal information.
  • Your yard sign: the QR code and PDF are made on your device.
  • Backups: "Export backup" saves a file of your Shingle data wherever you choose, such as Files or Drive. That file is yours. We never see it unless you send it to us. "Import backup" reads the file on your device only.
  • To delete everything, go to Settings and tap "Start over," or clear this site's data in your browser. Delete any backup files you saved yourself.

Buyer documents and proof of funds

Shingle doesn't upload, receive, or store buyer documents, pre-approval letters, or proof of funds. Buyers send those to you directly. Shingle only keeps the checkmarks you set, on your device.

Confirming your phone number

Before you sign, Shingle asks you to confirm your phone number with a one-time code.

  • What is sent to Twilio. Your phone number goes from your device to our server on Cloudflare, and from there to Twilio Verify, which texts you the code (or calls you if you ask). The code you type goes the same way so Twilio can check it. Twilio does not get your name, address, email, or anything else you enter. Those go only in the Formspree receipt described below.
  • Twilio. Twilio Inc. sends and checks the code for us through its Verify service. Twilio processes your phone number and delivery details under its own privacy notice. We don't use Twilio to send you anything else.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile. To stop automated abuse, the code step uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a check that runs in your browser instead of a puzzle. Turnstile collects technical signals from your browser and device to tell people from bots, and does not use them for advertising. When you ask for a code, our server sends the Turnstile result token and your internet (IP) address to Cloudflare to check it. See Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum.
  • What we keep. To limit how many codes can be sent, our server keeps short-lived counters in Cloudflare KV storage, keyed by phone number or internet address. A counter of code checks per phone number expires after 10 minutes. Counters of codes sent per phone number and per internet address expire after 1 hour. A daily total of codes sent, which is not tied to any person, expires after 24 hours. We don't store the code. Whether your phone was confirmed, and when, is added to your acknowledgement receipt.
  • Message and data rates may apply. You get one text, one resend, and then one call. We only contact the number you enter, and only when you ask for a code.

Text message codes

  • One-time security code only. The only texts Shingle sends are the one-time codes you ask for to confirm your phone. No marketing, no other messages.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Help. Reply HELP to a code text, or email michael@get5krealty.com.
  • Stop. Reply STOP to a code text to stop getting texts from that number.
  • Carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your disclaimer acknowledgement receipt

Before you use the app, each owner types their full name to accept the disclaimer, and you enter the property address, a phone number and an email. When you tap Accept and continue, Shingle sends a receipt to 5K Realty through Formspree with: the owner names, phone, email, property address, the date and time you accepted, the disclaimer version, and whether your phone number was confirmed and when. Formspree does not get the code. Nothing else you enter in the app is sent with the receipt.

We keep the receipt as a record that the disclaimer was accepted. 5K Realty keeps receipts for 5 years, then deletes them. Formspree emails it to 5K Realty and keeps a copy in its system. If you are offline, the app tries again when you reconnect, and you can send it from Settings.

Your own copy stays on your device. In Settings you can download it as a PDF or use Email me my copy, which opens your own email app with the details filled in. Shingle does not send that email for you.

To ask us to delete your receipt, email michael@get5krealty.com.

The "Talk to 5K Realty" form

  • When: only when you fill it out and tap Send.
  • What is sent:
  • What you type in: name, phone, email, property address, city or ZIP, state, what you need help with, the best time to reach you, and your message
  • Which page or screen you sent it from
  • A subject line made from your name and city or ZIP
  • Who processes it: Formspree (formspree.io) receives the form and emails it to 5K Realty. Formspree also keeps a copy of each submission in 5K Realty's Formspree account. Formspree's own policy: https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy
  • How we use it: only to contact you about your home. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.
  • Retention: 5K Realty keeps Talk to 5K Realty messages and disclaimer receipts for 5 years, then deletes them. You can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.
  • Deletion: email michael@get5krealty.com or call 704.649.6781 and ask us to delete your information. We'll delete the email and the Formspree copy, except anything we're required to keep by law.
  • Filling out the form doesn't create an agency relationship with 5K Realty, and it doesn't sign you up for calls or texts beyond replying to you.

Analytics

  • These pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: the Shingle page (get5krealty.com/shingle/), the Shingle terms (get5krealty.com/shingle/terms/), and the disclosures page (get5krealty.com/disclosures/). Cloudflare says it "does not use any client-side state, such as cookies or localStorage, to collect usage metrics" and doesn't fingerprint visitors. https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/
  • The Shingle app (/shingle/app/), this privacy policy, and the Shingle support page block the analytics script, so it doesn't run there.
  • The disclosures page also loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when that page opens. See Google's Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) handles the network requests when you load the site. https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

When you leave Shingle

Some buttons open other apps or sites, but only when you tap them: - Call, Text, and Email open your own phone, messages, or email app. Anything you send goes from your own account. - Links to official forms and notices (NC Real Estate Commission, SC LLR, EPA, HUD) open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If Shingle starts sending anything else off your device, this policy will be updated before that change goes live.
