Broker/Owner of 5K Realty, serving the Greater Charlotte area, NC/SC

Licensed since 2004. More than 20 years in new home sales with builders and developers across the Greater Charlotte market. NC and SC Broker in Charge since 2017.

I opened 5K Realty to do the work without the usual percentage commissions. Listing side: $5,000 or 1%, whichever is greater, paid at closing. Full service. Same work. More equity left for the seller. Offering a buyer’s agent commission is optional and up to the seller. The savings are still significant next to a more traditional listing, and each plan is tailored to the home and the seller.

The builder years are the useful part. I spent years representing big builders on new construction pricing, so pricing is where I start. I know how new construction is priced and how resale has to compete with it. We talk through a realistic high and low range, then set the list price based on your goals and timeline. I live here, so the advice is local across the Greater Charlotte area, NC/SC: South Charlotte, Ballantyne, Fort Mill, Lake Wylie, Tega Cay, Clover, Rock Hill, Indian Land, and York, Lancaster, and Chester County.
