VetResourceFinder Privacy Policy

Publisher: Ingold Group Properties, LLC (5K Realty)
Effective Date: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how VetResourceFinder (“the App”) handles information. The App is published by Ingold Group Properties, LLC, doing business as 5K Realty. VetResourceFinder is a free app. This build does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases. We do not operate a backend that collects your saved offers, suggestions, or reports.

2. Location (Near Me)

The Near Me feature may use your device location only while the App is open to search Apple Maps for nearby stores and businesses that offer veteran discounts (verified veteran discount brands, approximately within 25 miles). Location is not used in the background. The Online tab does not use your location.

3. Data on your device

Saved offers, suggestions, and reports stay on your device (and device backups). We do not receive that content on our servers.

4. No tracking SDKs

The App does not include analytics or tracking tools. The developer does not sell your personal information and does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in the App.

5. Third-party links

Some links in the App may open partner or other third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Some links may earn a partner fee if you choose to purchase through them. That does not change what the App collects. The App itself does not collect purchase data from those partners.
