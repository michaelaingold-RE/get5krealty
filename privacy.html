Privacy Policy

5K Realty · A trade name of Ingold Group Properties, LLC
Effective Date: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

5K Realty (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit our website (get5krealty.com), contact us, or use our real estate services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information — Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you fill out a form, call, text, or email us.
  • Property & Transaction Information — Details about homes you are interested in buying or selling, and information needed to provide real estate services.
  • Website Usage Information — IP address, browser type, pages visited, and general usage data collected through standard website analytics.
  • Communications — Records of emails, texts, calls, and form submissions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide real estate services
  • Schedule consultations, showings, and market analyses
  • Send relevant information about our services (you may opt out at any time)
  • Improve our website and client experience
  • Comply with legal and professional obligations

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these limited situations:

  • With service providers who help us operate our business (for example, email or form processing tools), under confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your consent or at your direction (such as sharing information with lenders, title companies, or other professionals involved in your transaction)

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update your information
  • Request that we delete your information (subject to legal and professional record-keeping requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications by replying “unsubscribe” or contacting us directly

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
