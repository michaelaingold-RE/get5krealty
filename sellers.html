Seller questions

Is this just an MLS dump?

No. Canopy MLS, photos, contracts, negotiation, and closing support are included.

How does buyer-agent pay work after the NAR settlement?

Fees are negotiated. NAR and the MLS do not set a standard commission. Offering a buyer's agent commission is optional and up to you, and it sits on top of the 5K listing-side fee. I typically recommend about half of my fee. If you offer it, it is also paid at closing.

Can I sell my home myself instead?

Yes. If you'd rather sell on your own, Shingle: The Seller Assist App is free from 5K Realty. It helps you plan showings, track which buyers have shown proof of funds with a simple checklist, review offers, and find the NC or SC disclosure forms. You can use it without hiring 5K Realty.

How do you set the list price?

We look at the home and the market together and talk through a realistic high and low range. Then, based on your goals and timeline, we decide where to start the list price. I spent years representing big builders on new construction pricing, so pricing is where I start.

Are there other optional services?

Yes, and they are discussed case by case. Other optional services, like staging or video, are paid directly to the vendor, not through 5K Realty. Even if you offer a buyer’s agent commission, the savings are still significant next to a more traditional listing, and each plan is tailored to the home and the seller.
